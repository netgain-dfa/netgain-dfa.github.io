Nature markets need a new kind of science-practitioner

NETGAIN is the UK's dedicated doctoral training programme for the science and practice of Biodiversity Net Gain and nature markets. We are building the talent and evidence base that an entire sector depends on, and there are several ways for your organisation to be part of it, from shaping research at no cost to sponsoring a PhD.

Why now

Biodiversity Net Gain is embedded in planning and development policy across the UK nations. Nature markets, from biodiversity credits to habitat banking, are scaling in response, and businesses of every kind face growing obligations to measure, disclose and address their biodiversity impacts and dependencies.

The IPBES 2026 Business and Biodiversity Assessment, the most authoritative global review of its kind, identified capacity and knowledge gaps as a primary barrier to action across all sectors. Methods for measuring business impacts and dependencies remain under-developed and under-adopted. Businesses want to act; the expertise to help them does not yet exist at scale.

Nature markets that deliver real environmental benefits do not belong to any single discipline. They need people who can work across ecology, economics, law, policy and society, yet doctoral training remains largely siloed. Regulators, developers, consultancies and NGOs report acute shortages of professionals who can integrate these perspectives, and the evidence base for BNG and market design is still in its infancy. Decisions worth billions of pounds, affecting irreplaceable habitats, are being made without the evidence or workforce they deserve. That is the gap NETGAIN was built to close.

What NETGAIN is

NETGAIN is a UKRI Doctoral Focal Award: a concentrated, challenge-led doctoral training programme co-created with industry, government and civil society. It brings together researchers from the Universities of St Andrews, Glasgow, Aberdeen and Durham, spanning ecology, statistics, economics, law, physical geography and social science.

Multidisciplinary
Ecology, statistics, economics, law, spatial science and social science within every cohort.
Co-created
Research and training shaped by partners through annual open sandpit events and ongoing collaboration.
Translational
Every PhD is designed to generate findings that practitioners and policymakers can use immediately.
Challenge-led
Projects address knowledge gaps identified by the sector, not by academic convention.
Open access
An evolving e-book synthesises student research into a freely available, standard-setting resource.
Inclusive
Widening participation and industry-sponsored pathways broaden access to doctoral training.

Ways to get involved

Every level of partnership is valued and formally acknowledged across NETGAIN communications and outputs.

Contribute, at no financial cost

Training and networking event
Host or co-deliver a training session that exposes the cohort to real-world challenges, tools and sector perspectives. A direct channel to tomorrow's talent.
NETGAIN seminar
Share your organisation's expertise, challenges or innovations in nature markets with our research community. Builds your profile and stimulates new research questions.
Experience internship, one month
Host a NETGAIN researcher for a short supervised placement. A low-commitment way to trial talent and co-generate insights.
Embedded internship, three to six months
A paid, task-focused placement that integrates a researcher into your organisation, co-producing directly applicable outputs.

Invest

Programme support
Support NETGAIN at a level that is meaningful to your organisation. We work with organisations of all sizes, and that flexibility is deliberate. Contributions enhance training capacity and reach.Flexible. We welcome a conversation about what works for you.

Project co-development and co-supervision
Co-design a project, contribute to supervision and help shape the research agenda. Join us

Our annual conference and co-creation day is a working session where partners, researchers and new contacts share findings, identify emerging needs and co-develop the next phase of work. Whether you are an existing partner, exploring involvement for the first time, or simply care about where nature markets are headed, it is the event to attend. Dates are posted on the seminars and events page.

You do not need to wait for the conference. We welcome enquiries at any point about any level of involvement at netgain@st-andrews.ac.uk. If someone in your team is ready for a doctorate, our industry-sponsored pathway enables professionals to undertake a PhD part-time.

NETGAIN's vision is a self-sustaining programme that positions the UK as a global leader in ecologically, economically and socially effective nature markets. Your involvement makes that possible.
