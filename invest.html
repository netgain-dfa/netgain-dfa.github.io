Invest

NETGAIN is a partner-driven programme. All of our research and training is co-developed with organisations in industry, government and civil society, and there are three ways to invest in it. Every investment is formally acknowledged on our partners page and across NETGAIN outputs.

  • Programme support

    Invest in NETGAIN itself and contribute to developing the talent pipeline the sector depends on. We recognise that not all organisations are equal: any level of investment is welcome, and all of it is fully acknowledged.

    Email us to find out more

  • Directly funded research

    Fund a PhD project developed by you together with academic experts. NETGAIN administers the studentship, the university and academics supervise, and your organisation sits on the supervisory team. Investment starts at about £15,000 a year for four years and NETGAIN covers all other costs. NETGAIN match funding is guaranteed provided the project is eligible, within remit and passes internal quality review.

    Email us to find out more

  • Project co-development

    Our standard route. Projects are co-developed between a team of academic experts and external partners on a challenge-led topic, with partners contributing to supervision and hosting placements. No financial commitment is required; these projects are competitively funded through our calls for proposals.

    Email us to find out more

Why now

Biodiversity Net Gain is embedded in planning and development policy across the UK nations. Nature markets, from biodiversity credits to habitat banking, are scaling in response, and businesses of every kind face growing obligations to measure, disclose and address their biodiversity impacts and dependencies.

The IPBES 2026 Business and Biodiversity Assessment identified capacity and knowledge gaps as a primary barrier to action across all sectors. Regulators, developers, consultancies and NGOs report acute shortages of professionals who can integrate ecological, economic and policy expertise, and the evidence base for BNG and market design is still in its infancy. Decisions worth billions of pounds, affecting irreplaceable habitats, are being made without the evidence or workforce they deserve. That is the gap NETGAIN was built to close.
