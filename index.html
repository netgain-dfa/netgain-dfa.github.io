The science and practice of nature markets

NETGAIN is a UKRI Doctoral Focal Award that trains researchers to make net gain work in practice. PhD students are supervised jointly across the Universities of St Andrews, Glasgow, Aberdeen and Durham, and every project is co-designed with a partner in government, industry or conservation. The work spans ecology, economics, law, data science and policy.

Information for prospective students, supervisors and partner organisations.
