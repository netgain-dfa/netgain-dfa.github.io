What NETGAIN does

Net gain is an approach to development that aims to leave the natural environment in a measurably better state than before. Making that promise real depends on markets, metrics and governance that are still being built.

NETGAIN trains the researchers who will build them. Our students work across ecology, economics, law, data science and policy, with supervisors at the Universities of St Andrews, Glasgow, Aberdeen and Durham, and with partners who put the research into practice.
