About NETGAIN

NETGAIN is a UKRI Doctoral Focal Award: a concentrated, challenge-led doctoral training programme for the science and practice of Biodiversity Net Gain and nature markets. It is delivered by the Universities of St Andrews, Glasgow, Aberdeen and Durham, spanning ecology, statistics, economics, law, physical geography and social science, and co-created with partners in industry, government and civil society. NETGAIN will train more than 45 PhD researchers across three cohorts, with over 60 external partners and more than £3M in financial support secured to date.

Multidisciplinary
Ecology, statistics, economics, law, spatial science and social science within every cohort.
Co-created
Research and training shaped by partners through annual open sandpit events and ongoing collaboration.
Translational
Every PhD is designed to generate findings that practitioners and policymakers can use immediately.
Challenge-led
Projects address knowledge gaps identified by the sector, not by academic convention.
Open access
An evolving e-book synthesises student research into a freely available, standard-setting resource.
Inclusive
Widening participation and industry-sponsored pathways broaden access to doctoral training.
